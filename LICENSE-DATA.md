## Source data

The raw datasets in `data/raw/` are derived from publicly available sources:

- `nba_player_stats.csv` and `nba_player_advanced.csv` were obtained via the
  unofficial NBA API client (https://github.com/swar/nba_api), which surfaces
  data from stats.nba.com. NBA.com data is owned by the NBA and is subject
  to its Terms of Service. We use this data under fair-use academic research.

- `nba_contracts_raw.csv` was obtained from Basketball Reference
  (https://www.basketball-reference.com/contracts/players.html). Data on
  Basketball Reference is owned by Sports Reference LLC and is subject to
  their Terms of Use. We use this data under fair-use academic research.

raw files in this repository only to support academic reproducibility of
